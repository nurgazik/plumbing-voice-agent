# Dry Run Plumbing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Dry Run Plumbing is a fictional plumbing company used as a public demonstration of an
AI phone receptionist. The demo is built and operated by Ray Nurgazy (nurgazy.com), an
independent software developer in Vancouver, British Columbia. No plumbing work is
performed and no payment is ever collected. Anyone may call the number and role-play a
plumbing problem to see how the system works.

## What we collect

When you call or text the demo number, we collect:

- Your mobile phone number (from caller ID).
- An audio recording and a text transcript of the call.
- Anything you say or type during the call: a name, a service address, a description
  of the problem, and any photo you choose to text in.
- Timestamps and system records of the quote, booking, and text messages generated.

We do not ask for and do not want payment card numbers, government ID numbers, health
information, or any other sensitive personal information. Please do not provide them.

## Why we collect it

Solely to run the demo conversation: to understand the problem you describe, produce a
preliminary price range, offer an appointment slot, and text you a summary. Aggregate,
de-identified information may be used to improve the agent's behaviour.

## Text messages

- Message frequency varies and depends on your interaction with the service. A typical
  demo call results in up to four messages (a preliminary quote, a booking confirmation,
  a wrap-up summary, and one follow-up).
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Reply STOP to any message to opt out of all further messages. Opt-outs are honoured
  immediately and permanently for that number.
- Reply HELP to any message, or email the address below, for help.

## We do not sell or share your mobile number

No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for
marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are
not shared with any third party for their own purposes. Phone numbers are shared only
with the service providers listed below, strictly to deliver the demo.

## Service providers

Call handling, messaging, storage, and AI processing are performed by third-party
providers acting on our behalf: Twilio (telephony and messaging), Retell AI (voice
conversation), Anthropic (AI models), n8n (workflow automation), Supabase (database),
Cal.com (scheduling), and HubSpot (contact records). These providers store and process
data on servers located in the United States. By using the demo you consent to your
information being processed outside Canada.

## Retention

Call recordings, transcripts, photos, and contact records are kept for up to 90 days,
then deleted. You may request earlier deletion of everything associated with your phone
number at any time, at no cost, by emailing the address below.

## Your choices

Under Canada's PIPEDA and British Columbia's PIPA you may ask what personal information
we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or withdraw consent and have it
deleted. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.
