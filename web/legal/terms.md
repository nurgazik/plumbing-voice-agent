Last updated: 13 September 2026

## What this is

Dry Run Plumbing is a fictional plumbing company. It exists as a public demonstration of
an AI phone receptionist, built and operated by Ray Nurgazy (nurgazy.com), an independent
software developer in Vancouver, British Columbia.

No plumbing work is performed. No technician is dispatched. No payment is ever requested
or collected. Prices, appointment slots, service areas, and policies mentioned during a
call are fictional and are not offers. Do not rely on anything the agent says for a real
plumbing situation.

If you have a real plumbing emergency, call a licensed plumber. If you smell gas, leave
the building and call your gas utility or 911.

## The call

Calls are answered by an automated AI agent, not a person. Calls are recorded, and the
agent says so at the start of every call. Continuing the call is your consent to being
recorded. The agent will tell you it is an AI if you ask.

Do not say anything during the call that you would not want recorded and stored. Do not
provide payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or anyone else's personal
information.

- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
